# Paz is open-source !

<mark style="color:$success;">For non-developers:</mark>

It is important to understand what open source means. The entire To-paz codebase is public and available for anyone to inspect. This means developers from all over the world can review the code, verify how the protocol works, and contribute to its development. It also means that you can inspect the project yourself using your favorite LLM before deciding to use the dApp.

Transparency should not depend on trust alone.

<mark style="color:$success;">For developers:</mark>

To-paz is distributed under the MIT License, and contributions of any size are greatly appreciated. I try to keep open issues for contributors of all skill levels, so even if you are completely new to zero-knowledge technology, your help is still valuable.

Do not hesitate to open issues, ask questions, or contribute for the first time.

A proper `CONTRIBUTING.md` is still a priority and already part of the [roadmap.md](roadmap.md "mention").
